## Figure 2.1 (optional): The four statements and how they connect

**Placement:** §2.2, after the four statements are introduced.

**Description:** A flow diagram showing the four statements in preparation order, with arrows indicating which figures flow between them. *Income Statement → net income → Statement of Owner's Equity → ending equity → Balance Sheet*. The Statement of Cash Flows sits beside the others with its ending cash figure flowing to the cash line on the Balance Sheet. Each statement labeled with its time orientation (period vs. snapshot).

**Pedagogical purpose:** Make the interrelation visible.

**Style notes:** Diagrammatic, four boxes with arrows. Color-code period vs. snapshot statements.
