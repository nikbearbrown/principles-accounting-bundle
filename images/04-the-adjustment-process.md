# Figure briefs — Chapter 4: The Adjustment Process

## Figure 4.1 (optional): Four types of adjusting entries

**Placement:** §4.4, summary table.

**Description:** Replicate the §4.4 summary table — four rows (Prepaid expense, Unearned revenue, Accrued expense, Accrued revenue), four columns (Type, Cash event, Earning/consumption event, Adjusting entry pattern).

**Pedagogical purpose:** Reference table students return to throughout the chapter and into Chapter 5.

**Style notes:** Clean tabular. Color-code deferrals vs accruals.

## Figure 4.2 (optional): Time-line of a deferral vs an accrual

**Placement:** §4.4 introduction.

**Description:** Two horizontal time lines. Top: a deferral — cash event at left (start of period), earning/consumption gradually across the period, adjusting entry at right (period end). Bottom: an accrual — earning/consumption gradually across the period, adjusting entry at right (period end), cash event later (next period). Each diagram shows the original journal entry on the cash event and the adjusting entry at period end.

**Pedagogical purpose:** Make the time-decoupling visible.

**Style notes:** Diagrammatic, two-track time-line.

## Figure 4.3 (optional): Supreme Cleaners adjusted trial balance

**Placement:** §4.5.

**Description:** Three-column working paper showing unadjusted trial balance, adjusting entries, adjusted trial balance — the standard ten-column worksheet format common in introductory accounting practice.

**Pedagogical purpose:** Show the full adjustment process visually with all five adjustments propagating through.

**Style notes:** Standard worksheet format; clean tabular.
