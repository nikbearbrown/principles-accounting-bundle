## About the Author

**Nik Bear Brown** teaches at Northeastern University and advises early-stage AI startups through [Bear Brown & Company](https://www.bearbrown.co/). His MBA from Northeastern brings the accounting foundations this book teaches into his work with founders.

He is the founder of [Humanitarians AI](https://www.humanitarians.ai/), a 501(c)(3) whose nonprofit accounting structure is part of his working practice, and the author of the *with LLMs* textbook series.
