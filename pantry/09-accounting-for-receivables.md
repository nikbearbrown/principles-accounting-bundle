# Pantry — Chapter 9: Accounting for Receivables

## Scenes
- $4,000 net-30 credit sale waiting in AR for 30 days. §9.1.
- Aging schedule with five buckets producing $26,000 estimated allowance. §9.4.
- 90-day 8% $5,000 note from April 1 with period-end straddle. §9.5.

## Trade-offs named
- Allowance method buys matching at the cost of estimation. §9.3.
- Percentage-of-sales emphasizes income statement; aging emphasizes balance sheet. §9.4.

## Worked examples
- $1,200,000 credit sales × 2% = $24,000 BDE. §9.7 ex 4.
- Aging-method true-up entry from $4,000 to $26,000 allowance. §9.4.
- 90-day note interest = $5,000 × 8% × 90/360 = $100. §9.5.

## Reusable terms
Accounts receivable; notes receivable; allowance for doubtful accounts; net realizable value; direct write-off; allowance method; percentage-of-sales; percentage-of-receivables; aging schedule; principal; maturity; dishonored note; AR turnover; DSO.
