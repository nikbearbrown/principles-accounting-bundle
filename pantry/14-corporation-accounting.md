# Pantry — Chapter 14: Corporation Accounting
## Worked examples
- 1,000 shares $1 par at $25 → Common Stock $1,000, APIC $24,000
- 100 shares treasury at $40 cost
- 5% stock dividend on 2M shares at $25 market
## Reusable terms
Common stock; preferred stock (cumulative, convertible, callable, participating); par value; no-par; APIC; treasury stock (cost method); contra-equity; authorized shares; issued shares; outstanding shares; retained earnings; cash dividend; stock dividend (small <25% / large >25%); stock split; declaration date / record date / payment date; dividends in arrears; EPS (basic / diluted); book value per share; price-to-book ratio; legal capital doctrine.
