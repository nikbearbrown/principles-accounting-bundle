# Pantry — Chapter 7: Accounting Information Systems

## Scenes

- **Regional grocery chain processing 12,000 transactions/Saturday across 85 stores** — §7.1 cold open. Reusable for any chapter on enterprise-scale operations.
- **Single grocery transaction traced through full AIS** — §7.8. Reusable as canonical illustration of the input-process-output-storage pipeline.

## Trade-offs named

- **Computerization buys speed, scale, and consistency at the cost of opacity and dependency.** §7.2.
- **Special journals buy recording efficiency at the cost of requiring transaction classification at recording.** §7.3.
- **Tighter controls buy fraud and error prevention at the cost of operational friction.** §7.5.

## Reusable terms

Input, processing, output, storage; sales journal; cash receipts journal; cash disbursements journal; purchases journal; subsidiary ledger; control account; small-business AIS (QuickBooks); mid-market AIS (NetSuite, Sage Intacct); ERP (SAP, Oracle, Workday); access controls; edit checks; audit trail.
