# Pantry — Chapter 10: Inventory

## Trade-offs named
- FIFO buys balance-sheet realism at the cost of income-statement looseness in inflation. §10.2.
- LIFO buys income-statement matching at the cost of balance-sheet staleness. §10.2.
- LIFO conformity rule ties tax and reporting choices together. §10.3.
