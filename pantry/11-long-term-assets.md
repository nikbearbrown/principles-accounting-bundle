# Pantry — Chapter 11: Long-Term Assets

## Scenes
- Delivery truck $40,700 over 7 years to $5,000 salvage. §11.1.

## Trade-offs named
- Straight-line vs accelerated: simplicity vs better matching of early-life depreciation. §11.3.
- Capitalize vs expense: matching to benefited periods vs administrative simplicity. §11.2.
- Indefinite-life intangibles: not amortized but require impairment testing. §11.6.

## Worked examples
- Truck straight-line: $5,100/year.
- Truck DDB: $11,640 → $8,313 → $5,937...
- Truck units-of-production: $0.102/mile.
- Truck disposal at $4,800 (loss $200) or $5,500 (gain $500).
- Timber depletion: $0.02/board foot.
